# Deep Learning & ML From Scratch: Learning Roadmap

Welcome to your Deep Learning journey! To truly understand "Deep Learning," we will build everything from the ground up. This means minimal use of high-level libraries like Keras/PyTorch at the start, focusing instead on **NumPy** to understand the math behind the magic.

## Phase 1: The Foundations (Week 1)
*   **Linear Algebra**: Vectors, Matrices, Matrix Multiplication, Dot Products.
*   **Calculus**: Derivatives, Chain Rule (Crucial for Backpropagation).
*   **Environment Setup**: Setting up Python, NumPy, and Matplotlib.

## Phase 2: Classical Machine Learning from Scratch (Week 2)
*   **Linear Regression**: The "Hello World" of ML. Implementing Gradient Descent.
*   **Logistic Regression**: Moving to classification. Sigmoid functions.
*   **Loss Functions**: Mean Squared Error (MSE) vs. Binary Cross-Entropy.

## Phase 3: The Neural Network Core (Week 3-4)
*   **The Perceptron**: The building block of AI.
*   **Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)**:
    *   Forward Propagation.
    *   Backpropagation (The Hard Part).
    *   Implementing a simple 3-layer network in pure NumPy.
*   **Activation Functions**: ReLU, Tanh, Sigmoid.

## Phase 4: Modern Deep Learning (Week 5+)
*   **Introduction to PyTorch**: Moving from manual math to automated gradients.
*   **Computer Vision**: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).
*   **NLP & Sequences**: RNNs and the Power of Transformers.
