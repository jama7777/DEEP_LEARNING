# 🗺️ Pro Roadmap V2: The "Verbal" Path (Language Mastery)
This roadmap focuses strictly on **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and building **Small Language Models (SLMs)**.

---

## 🟢 Phase 4: Verbal Foundations (Words as Vectors)
*Current Focus: Turning human language into math.*

1.  **Word Embeddings**: How do we map "King" and "Queen" into a 2D space?
2.  **The Vocabulary Problem**: Tokenization and how we break sentences into "Lego blocks."
3.  **One-Hot Words**: Using our `Dense` layer to predict the next word in a simple 3-word sentence.

## 🟡 Phase 5: Sequence & Memory (The Context Problem)
*How to remember what was said before.*

1.  **Recurrent Logic (RNNs)**: Adding a "Loop" to our modular framework so it has a memory.
2.  **Vanishing Gradients in Text**: Why it's hard for a network to remember the start of a long paragraph.
3.  **LSTMs/GRUs**: The "Long-term Memory" blocks that fixed the language problem.

## 🔴 Phase 6: The Attention Revolution (Transformers)
*How models focus on what matters.*

1.  **The Attention Mechanism**: Why some words are more important than others in a sentence.
2.  **Positional Encoding**: How the network knows where a word sits in a sentence without a loop.
3.  **The SLM Architecture**: Designing the "Brain" for your Enterprise Small Language Model.

## 🎓 Phase 7: Building the Nature SLM
*Training on real language.*

1.  **Dataset Creation**: Building a custom "Nature" text dataset for training.
2.  **GPU Training**: Using Apple Silicon to train your model on thousands of sentences.
3.  **Inference**: Talking to your model and watching it generate its first verbal responses.

---
**Philosophy**: We ignore pixels and vision. We focus entirely on the **Verbal** logic of sequences, context, and meaning.
